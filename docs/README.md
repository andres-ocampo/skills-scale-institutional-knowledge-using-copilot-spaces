# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a customer-focused, iterative lifecycle with clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and regular stakeholder communication. The process begins with initiation to validate the business need, define success measures, identify stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed. Planning turns approved work into prioritized, estimable backlog items with acceptance criteria, milestones, dependencies, risks, and a Definition of Done. During execution, teams track work on a project board, deliver and review small increments, test changes, monitor progress, and escalate blockers. Release guidance covers readiness checks, deployment verification, communication, and rollback. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture learning and turn it into owned improvement actions. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communications; Product Managers guide outcomes, prioritization, and success measures; developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute to delivery and validation.

## Project Management Lifecycle
1. Initiation: validate the problem, define success metrics, align stakeholders, and decide whether the work should continue.
2. Planning: turn the approved initiative into a backlog, timeline, milestones, and clear ownership.
3. Execution: build, review, test, and track delivery through frequent team communication and progress monitoring.
4. Release & Deployment: verify readiness, deploy with safety checks, and communicate status to stakeholders.
5. Close & Retrospective: capture lessons learned and convert improvements into action items.
